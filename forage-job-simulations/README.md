# Forage Job Simulations

> Completed virtual experience programs hosted on Forage, simulating real-world roles across various industries.

---

## Overview

These simulations are designed by top global companies to reflect actual day-to-day tasks performed in roles across cybersecurity, software engineering, data analytics, finance, and more.  
Each program builds practical skills and offers insight into industry workflows, tools, and professional expectations.

---

## Key Highlights

- Real-world simulations across technical and business domains  
- Task-based experiences developed by companies like Mastercard  
- Practice with tools, workflows, and problem-solving used in real jobs  
- Great for showcasing practical skills and workplace readiness
